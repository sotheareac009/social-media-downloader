Media Downloader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Media Downloader ("the app") is a desktop application that downloads publicly accessible media and, optionally, connects your own social media accounts so you can download or publish your own content. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app runs on your own computer, and your account credentials stay on your computer.

What the app stores, and where

  • Account sessions and tokens (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram) are stored only on your own device — in your operating system's secure store or in owner-only files in the app's local data folder. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. We do not operate a server that receives them.
  • Your display name and profile picture may be fetched from a connected platform and shown in the app. This is stored locally only, to label the connected account.
  • Downloaded files are saved to a folder you choose on your own computer. They are never sent anywhere.

How connected accounts are used

  • Signing in authenticates you directly with the platform. The app never sees your password.
  • A connected account is used only for actions you initiate in the app — such as downloading your own media, or publishing content you choose to a Facebook Page you manage.
  • The app requests only the permissions needed for those actions and uses the resulting access tokens solely to make those requests on your behalf.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking on your account data.
  • We have no server that stores your credentials or content.

Removing your data

You can disconnect any account inside the app at any time, which deletes its stored session and profile from your device. You can also revoke the app's access from the platform's own settings (for example, Facebook → Settings → Business Integrations). Uninstalling the app removes its local data.
