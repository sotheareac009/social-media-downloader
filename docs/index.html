Media Downloader

A desktop app for downloading and publishing your own social media content.

Media Downloader is a macOS and Windows desktop application. It downloads publicly accessible videos and photos, and can connect your own accounts on TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Telegram so you can download or publish your own content. Everything runs locally on your computer — there is no server, and your account credentials never leave your device.

What it does

  • Download public videos from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram.
  • Connect your own account to download or publish your own content.
  • Choose quality up to 8K, and pick where files are saved.
